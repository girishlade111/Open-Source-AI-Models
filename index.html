The Sonic Frontier

The audio domain features highly specialized models for distinct tasks like speech synthesis, music generation, and recognition. Unlike LLMs, the "best" audio model is entirely dependent on the specific application, from low-latency TTS to high-fidelity voice cloning.

The Moving Image

Video generation is the most computationally demanding frontier. Progress is defined by the "computational cliff"—the immense hardware required to generate coherent video, which limits widespread adoption. The leading models showcase architectural innovation, but accessibility remains the key challenge.

The Computational Cliff

The primary barrier in open-source video is hardware. Generating high-quality video often requires server-grade GPUs with massive amounts of VRAM, far exceeding typical consumer hardware.

The AI Co-Pilot

Code generation models are now essential developer tools. The key distinction among top models isn't just benchmark scores, but their effectiveness in different coding scenarios: writing new code ("greenfield") versus modifying existing codebases ("brownfield").

The Convergence

Multimodal models bridge the gap between vision and language, reasoning over images and text simultaneously. The power of these models is fundamentally anchored to the quality of their underlying LLM, which provides the core cognitive engine.
